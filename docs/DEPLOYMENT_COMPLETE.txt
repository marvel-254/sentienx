DASHBOARD DATA & METRICS

Account Information Displayed:
──────────────────────────────
• Account Balance: Real-time balance from Deriv
• Equity: Current account equity
• Free Margin: Available for trading
• Margin Level: Percentage of used margin
• Open Positions: Count of active trades
• Total Trades: Lifetime trade count

Performance Metrics:
────────────────────
• Win Rate: Percentage of winning trades
• Profit Factor: Ratio of wins to losses
• Total P&L: Cumulative profit/loss
• Daily P&L: Today's profit/loss
• Best Trade: Largest winning trade
• Worst Trade: Largest losing trade
• Average Trade: Mean P&L per trade
• Sharpe Ratio: Risk-adjusted returns
• Max Drawdown: Largest peak-to-trough decline

Trading Details:
────────────────
• Symbol: EUR/USD, GBP/USD, USD/JPY
• Direction: BUY or SELL
• Entry Price: Price when trade opened
• Exit Price: Price when trade closed
• Volume: Lot size/contract size
• Profit/Loss: Dollar amount made/lost
• Status: Open or Closed
• Timestamp: When trade was executed
• Strategy: Which strategy generated signal

API DOCUMENTATION (Built-In)

Available Endpoints:
────────────────────
Account Endpoints:
  GET  /api/account/balance          - Current balance
  GET  /api/account/equity            - Current equity
  GET  /api/account/positions         - Open positions
  GET  /api/account/trades            - Trade history

Market Data Endpoints:
  GET  /api/market/price/{symbol}     - Current price
  GET  /api/market/sentiment/{symbol} - Market sentiment
  GET  /api/market/news               - Latest news
  GET  /api/market/economic-calendar  - Economic events

Trading Endpoints:
  POST /api/trade/buy                 - Place buy order
  POST /api/trade/sell                - Place sell order
  POST /api/trade/close/{position_id} - Close position
  GET  /api/trade/signals             - Trading signals

System Endpoints:
  GET  /api/health                    - Health check
  GET  /api/status                    - System status

Example Request:
─────────────────
curl -X GET http://localhost:8080/api/account/balance \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer ***REMOVED***"
